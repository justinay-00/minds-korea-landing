AI Agents and Persistent Memory Features

We provide AI agents to assist with your tasks. Those agents use AI models to understand, analyse and respond to your inputs, which may include commands, questions, prompts, information and content that you submit or upload or to which you grant access in order to use our services such as social media or wallet and trading information (collectively, “AI Inputs”), and execute tasks on your behalf. Those AI Inputs may include personal data.

The AI agents have persistent memory which means those agents retain key information from your interactions, such as preferences, configurations or attributes that you share with or create for those agents or that can be derived from your usage patterns or interactions with those agents (“Memory Data”). That Memory Data will enable the AI agents to deliver more personalised and customised responses and to maintain context.

Outputs generated by the AI agents are produced by automated systems and may be inaccurate or incomplete. Where an output contains information about you, it may represent an inference generated by a model rather than a statement of verified fact.

Do not include sensitive or special category personal data (such as information about health, biometrics, racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, sexual life or criminal records) about yourself or any other person in your AI Inputs unless it is strictly necessary. We do not request such data and do not use it for any purpose other than providing the services at your direction. If you choose to include such data in your AI Inputs, you are responsible for ensuring that you are entitled to provide it.

You are reminded that once you open an account to use the agentic AI services on the Website, you will be required to provide AI Inputs which will be processed by AI agents with persistent memory, creating Memory Data. When you create an account, you will be asked to accept the Terms of Use and this Privacy Policy, which explains how we process your personal data. Because persistent memory is a core part of the agentic AI services, we process the personal data contained in your AI Inputs and Memory Data on the basis that it is necessary for the performance of our contract with you (that is, to provide the services you have asked for), and not on the basis of your consent. This means that you cannot use the agentic AI services without this processing; if you do not wish your personal data to be processed in this way, you should not use those services. Where we rely on your consent for a separate, optional purpose (for example, marketing), we will ask for that consent separately, and you may withdraw it at any time without affecting your ability to use the core services.

IMPORTANT NOTICE REGARDING PERSISTENT MEMORY AND DATA DELETION

The persistent memory feature is a core component of an AI agent. Once you deploy an AI agent through our platform, that agent will automatically retain Memory Data derived from your AI Inputs and interactions on a continuous basis. This feature cannot be switched off.

This means that:

  1. Memory Data may contain personal data, including information you provide directly and information derived from your usage patterns and interactions with your agents.
  2. Memory Data is retained and processed across sessions, agent runs, and platform restarts. It persists until your account is terminated and, in some cases, beyond termination, subject to the limitations described below.
  3. Agents may share Memory Data with other agents deployed through your account for the purposes of coordinating tasks. Please exercise caution about what personal data you include in your AI Inputs.

If you do not wish your personal data to be processed in this manner, you should not use the Website.

While you have a right to request deletion of your personal data (as described in the "Your Rights" section of this Policy), the technical nature of persistent memory means that complete erasure of all Memory Data cannot be guaranteed in all cases. Specifically:

  1. Certain Memory Data may be stored in compressed, encoded, or vector-embedded form within third-party large language model or infrastructure environments that are not fully within our control or accessible to us for the purposes of targeted deletion.
  2. We do not currently provide an in-app tool to view or selectively delete individual items of Memory Data. If, in the course of a conversation, you instruct an AI agent to "forget" or disregard specific information, that instruction operates only within that conversation or agent session and does not constitute a verified deletion of the underlying data from our (or any third-party provider’s) storage systems. To have your Memory Data deleted, you may close your Account as described below. Before doing so, you may export your Memory Data as part of your Agent Materials, as described in the section headed “Your Agent and Agent Exports”.
  3. Where Memory Data has been incorporated into model inference outputs, infrastructure environments or execution logs held by third-party LLM providers, we may be technically unable to procure its deletion from those providers' systems, but we will use our reasonable endeavours to procure the pseudonymization or anonymization of such data that cannot be deleted from third-party systems.

Session recordings are made only if you have accepted analytics cookies, and are deleted after 30 days. You can withdraw your consent at any time through our cookie settings.

On our Website, we use cookies and URL parameters to track whether you have accessed our Website via an affiliate or referral link. These tracking technologies capture a referral identifier at the point of click and associate it with your account upon sign-up. Affiliate tracking cookies on the Website remain active for a period of 30 days from the date of the referral click.
